# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant][covenant], version 2.1, as its
code of conduct. The full text is published at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

In short: participants are expected to behave respectfully and in good faith.
Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning or exclusionary conduct are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **conduct@playgroundlogic.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will acknowledge a report within
5 business days and will not disclose the reporter's identity without their
consent.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take any action they judge appropriate and proportionate, up to and including a
permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the
code of conduct in good faith may face repercussions as determined by other
project leaders.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces and also when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
